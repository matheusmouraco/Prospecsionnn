About Brickox

At Brickox, our mission is to empower clients to realize their full potential through smart planning, engineering expertise, and practical construction solutions.

Our vision is to deliver lasting impact by building high-quality projects that combine innovation, safety, and operational excellence.

Over the years, Brickox has partnered with clients across residential, commercial, and infrastructure sectors to improve efficiency, drive growth, and successfully deliver complex construction projects. We are now looking for skilled professionals to join us in shaping landmark developments and long-term success stories. Culturally, we are a team of experienced engineers, project managers, and construction specialists, united by a commitment to quality, collaboration, and results that truly stand the test of time. This role is based at our [City, State] office, offering a hybrid work model that balances on-site collaboration with flexible remote or full-time arrangements, depending on project requirements.

The role

We are seeking a world-class construction professional to lead end-to-end project initiatives, oversee planning and cost structures, and help make Brickox an even stronger and more trusted construction partner for our clients.

What you’ll do

Project & Delivery Initiatives

  • Lead major construction initiatives on a quarterly basis, ensuring timely execution and measurable impact.
  • Own project positioning, service structuring, cost planning, and delivery strategy across key developments.

Communication & Project Updates

  • Own and manage regular project updates, milestones, and internal reporting on a weekly and monthly basis.
  • Share clear, high-quality project insights with internal teams and stakeholders on a consistent schedule.

Communication & Project Updates

  • Develop long-term strategies for cost planning, service structuring, and project packaging.
  • Collaborate with engineering, procurement, and operations teams to continuously refine construction offerings and delivery models.

Who you are

  • 4+ years of experience in construction project management, planning, or a related role.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to coordinate effectively across teams and stakeholders.
  • Proven project management and execution capability, able to manage multiple workstreams and meet deadlines.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary with performance-based incentives.
  • Paid time off, sick leave, and public holidays, supporting work–life balance.
  • Comprehensive benefits, including health, dental, and vision coverage, along with retirement plans where applicable.
  • Flexible work arrangements, including site-based, office, and hybrid options depending on role requirements.
  • An inclusive and diverse workplace, where people are respected, supported, and empowered to grow.

Compensation

  • $168-$250k per year, plus meaningful equity.

Our values

  • Lead by Example: We strive for excellence in every project. At Brickox, every team member takes ownership and helps others reach their full potential. Being a Brickox team member means being both a collaborator and a trusted partner to our clients.
  • Take Responsibility: We act as owners. When we see an opportunity to improve a process, a project, or a design, we step up and lead the change.
  • Work as a Team: Construction is a team effort. We achieve the best results when we collaborate, share knowledge, and support one another. Great projects are built by great teams, and we hold ourselves to the highest standards.
  • Be Transparent: We are open in communication, decision-making, and collaboration. Sharing information, ideas, and diverse perspectives makes us stronger and helps us deliver exceptional outcomes.
  • Be kind: We can be honest and kind. We can have high standards and be kind. We can say no and be kind. Kindness can vary across cultures, upbringings, and languages - but we try our best to be kind.
