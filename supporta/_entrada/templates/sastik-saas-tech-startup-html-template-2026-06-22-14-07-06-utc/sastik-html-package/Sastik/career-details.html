Business Development Executive

Turning conversations into consistent revenue growth

About Company

Thousands of companies trust us to streamline operations and drive growth. Our platform helps teams manage and automate processes without complexity. We’re a team of builders and collaborators who value simplicity, speed, and creating products that truly make an impact.

The Role

You will take ownership of generating new business opportunities, engaging with prospects, and building a strong pipeline through consistent outreach and relationship-building.

You will work closely with internal teams to expand market reach, convert prospects into customers, and contribute to the overall growth of the business.

What You’ll Do

  • Identify and pursue new business opportunities
  • Build and maintain client relationships
  • Execute outreach campaigns across multiple channels
  • Present and promote product solutions
  • Collaborate with marketing and sales teams
  • Manage leads and update CRM systems

What We’re Looking For

  • 1–3 years of experience in business development or sales
  • Strong communication and negotiation skills
  • Experience working with SaaS or tech products (preferred)
  • Familiarity with CRM tools
  • Self-driven and goal-oriented mindset

Nice to Have

  • Experience working in B2B or SaaS sales environments
  • Strong networking skills with an existing industry connection base
  • Familiarity with sales automation and outreach tools
  • Ability to analyze data and optimize outreach strategies

Benefits

Enjoy a competitive salary with performance-based bonuses, work in a flexible environment that supports your lifestyle, grow your career through continuous opportunities, and be part of a collaborative team culture that values trust and shared success.
