  • How does Sastik help desk work?

    Sastik Help Desk centralizes customer conversations in a shared inbox, letting your team manage and respond to email and live chat tickets, collaborate internally, automate tasks, and track performance all from one platform.

  • Can I try Sastik for free?

    Yes. You can start a free trial of Sastik Help Desk with no credit card required. Explore core features like ticket management, shared inbox, and automation to see how it works for your team before upgrading.

  • Is my customer and support data secure?

    Yes. Sastik uses industry-standard security practices to protect your data. All customer and support information is securely stored, and access controls ensure only authorized team members can view or manage sensitive data.

  • Can my team collaborate on tickets?

    Absolutely. Team members can assign tickets, add internal notes, and work together in real time. This makes collaboration seamless and helps resolve customer issues faster and more accurately across teams.

  • Does it support email and live chat?

    Yes. Sastik fully supports both email and live chat, allowing you to easily manage all customer conversations efficiently from one single centralized inbox so nothing ever gets missed or overlooked.

  • How quickly can my team get started?

    Your team can get started in just a few short and simple minutes without any hassle. Setup is simple, with no complex configuration required, so you can begin receiving and responding to customer requests right away.
