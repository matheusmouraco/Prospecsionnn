Two menus

One kitchen, two services. From noon the brasserie sends out shellfish on ice, pasta from the pass and the fish that came in at dawn. From five the bar takes the terrace: spritz, vermouth and small plates until the last glass.

Explore the menus

Lunch thatruns intothe evening

Ombrelle began as six tables and two parasols on Linden Square. The parasols are bigger now and the kitchen is open to the room, but the habit stayed: people come for lunch, order another coffee and are still here when the lamps go on.

Ombrelle moments

Follow us

Noon to midnight

The house changes five times a day. The card, the light and the music follow the clock; the tables stay where they are.

  1. 12:00–15:00

    Lunch

    The full brasserie card. Shellfish on ice, a pasta from the pass, the fish of the day with whatever the market sent.

  2. 15:00–17:00

    Coffee hour

    Espresso, a slice of lemon tart, the terrace in half shade. The kitchen rests; the coffee keeps going.

  3. 17:00–19:30

    Aperitivo

    Spritz, vermouth on ice, olives warmed with orange zest and a plate of fried sardines to share.

  4. 19:30–23:00

    Dinner

    The card again, now by candlelight. Book ahead for the terrace on warm nights.

  5. 23:00–00:00

    Last glass

    The kitchen closes and the bar keeps a short card of cheese, anchovy toast and something to finish the night.

Under theparasols

Twelve parasols on the square, a room of sixty behind the glass and a counter for those who eat alone. The back room seats twenty for birthdays, long lunches and the occasional wedding.
