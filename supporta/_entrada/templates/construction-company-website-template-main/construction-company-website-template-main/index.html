A fixed price, from a measured survey.

03( Survey )Before we quote, two of us spend half a day in your house. What follows is a drawing, a schedule and one number.

  1. 01( Measure )

    Every wall, opening and floor level, drawn to the quarter inch and sent to you as a PDF.

  2. 02( Open up )

    With your permission we lift a board or two: joists and wiring decide the price, not guesses.

  3. 03( Price )

    One number per room, itemised and valid for sixty days. Changes are priced before they are built.

  4. 04( Cost )

    The survey costs $450 and comes off the job if you go ahead with us.

07( Promise )Three things we put in writing and sign with the contract.

  • Fixed price

    The number on your survey is the number on the last invoice, unless you change the plan.

  • Fixed date

    You get a handover date before we start. If we miss it, we pay you $250 for every working day.

  • Ten years

    Structure and waterproofing are guaranteed for ten years, and the guarantee moves with the house if you sell.

Before you call

10( Questions )The six things people ask us first.

( Area )Where do you work?

Brooklyn and the western half of Queens. For a whole-house job we also travel to Manhattan and Jersey City.

( Size )What is the smallest job you take?

A single bathroom. We do not take repairs or handyman work; we are not set up to do them well.

( Permits )Who deals with permits and inspections?

We do. Our expediter files the drawings, books every inspection and keeps the permit in your window.

( Living in )Can we stay in the house?

For kitchens and bathrooms, usually yes. We seal off the work area with dust walls and leave you a temporary sink.

( Payment )How do payments work?

A ten percent deposit, then a payment as each stage passes inspection. Never ahead of the work.

( Start )How soon can you start?

Surveys within two weeks of your call. Building usually starts six to ten weeks after you sign.
