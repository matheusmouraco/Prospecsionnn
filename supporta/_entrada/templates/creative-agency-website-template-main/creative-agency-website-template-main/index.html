Brief the house

From first line to last frame

Four stages, one crew. The people who write the sentence a campaign has to earn are the ones who sign off its last asset.

  1. 1

    Write

    We start with what should remain: one sentence the whole campaign has to earn. Strategy, concept, casting and the material board are written against it.

  2. 2

    Build

    Sets, wardrobe and light are designed in our own workshop, tested on the stage and signed off before a single crew call.

  3. 3

    Shoot

    One crew captures the film, the stills and the social cut-downs on the same days, under the same light, with the same eye.

  4. 4

    Finish & scale

    Edit, colour, retouch and 3D happen down the corridor; the content factory then adapts every asset for every market, format and shelf.
