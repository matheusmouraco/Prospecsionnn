# Fonts

Both fonts are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org).

- `fraunces-300.woff2`, `fraunces-300-italic.woff2`: Fraunces (Undercase Type, Phaedra Charles, Flavia Zimbardi), Latin subset from Google Fonts, instanced with fontTools to weight 300 and optical size 14 to 144.
- `inter-400-500.woff2`: Inter (Rasmus Andersson), Latin subset from Google Fonts, weight axis limited to 400 to 500.

Instancing cuts the payload from about 197 KB to 106 KB without changing how the type looks at the weights used.
